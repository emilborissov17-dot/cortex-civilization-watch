# EDUCATION CULTURE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-24

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Significant gaps in AI and digital literacy among K-12 students worldwide, leading to unequal preparation for future work and civic participation.

## Root Cause
Absence of universally adopted AI curriculum standards and insufficient teacher training, compounded by reliance on unverified educational content and assumptions about teacher motivations.

## Proposed Actions
- **Adopt UNESCO’s AI Competency Framework as a mandatory component of national K-12 curricula and fund a global teacher‑training program on AI literacy.** — Increase the percentage of schools meeting the AI literacy benchmark from current baseline to >80% within 5 years, measured by standardized AI literacy assessments.
- **Scale the Learning Commons open platform with vetted, standards‑aligned AI teaching resources and require its integration in at least 50% of public schools.** — Number of schools using the platform and number of lessons delivered; target 1 million AI lessons deployed within 3 years.
