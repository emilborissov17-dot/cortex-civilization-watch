# ENERGY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-24

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Legacy electricity grid infrastructure cannot accommodate the rapid increase of variable renewable energy sources, causing curtailment, reliability issues, and slowing the clean energy transition.

## Root Cause
The grid was originally designed for centralized, dispatchable generation; insufficient investment, outdated regulations, and utility resistance have prevented necessary upgrades such as flexible transmission, smart‑grid controls, and large‑scale storage.

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement a coordinated national grid‑modernization program that funds smart‑grid technologies, high‑capacity transmission lines, and grid‑scale storage, with performance‑based contracts for utilities.** — Reduce renewable curtailment to <5% of total renewable generation and cut grid congestion incidents by 30% within five years.
- **Revise utility rate structures and performance metrics to reward integration of renewables and penalize excessive emissions or delays in grid upgrades.** — Achieve a 15% improvement in utility clean‑energy scores (e.g., Sierra Club’s Dirty Truth rating) across the top 20 utilities within three years.
