## Problem
Deforestation and habitat loss lead to significant biodiversity loss, with an area the size of a soccer field being clear-cut every minute in the Amazon basin

## Root Cause
Human activities such as deforestation, driven by economic interests and lack of effective conservation policies, are the primary cause of biodiversity loss

- **Implement and enforce stricter conservation policies and protected areas** — Reduce deforestation rates by 50% within the next 5 years
