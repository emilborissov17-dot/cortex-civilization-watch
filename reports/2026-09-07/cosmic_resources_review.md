## Problem
Absence of a binding international regulatory framework for asteroid and space resource mining, leading to speculative investment and potential unsustainable exploitation.

## Root Cause
National legislation on space resources has stalled (e.g., the 2015 U.S. Space Resource Exploration and Utilization Act never became law) and there is no coordinated global treaty, allowing hype to outpace technical readiness and environmental safeguards.

## Proposed Actions
- ** convene a UN-led working group to draft a Space Resource Sustainability Treaty that defines permissible extraction methods, environmental impact assessments, and benefit‑sharing mechanisms.** —  treaty text approved by at least 75% of UN member states within 3 years.
- **establish an industry certification program for space mining projects that verifies compliance with the treaty’s sustainability criteria.** —  10 certified projects operating by 5 years after treaty adoption.
