# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-07

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Accelerating habitat destruction is simultaneously driving massive biodiversity loss and the extinction of indigenous languages.

## Root Cause
Unsustainable land-use practices driven by economic expansion, weak regulatory frameworks, and insufficient recognition of indigenous stewardship lead to deforestation, fragmentation, and degradation of natural habitats.

## Proposed Actions
- **Create and empower a Global Wildlife Health Authority to coordinate cross‑border habitat protection, monitor biodiversity metrics, and integrate cultural‑heritage safeguards.** — By 2028, establish the Authority with legal mandate in at least 30 countries and achieve a 15% reduction in net habitat loss in its jurisdictions.
- **Legislate and fund co‑managed protected areas with indigenous peoples, linking habitat conservation funding to language preservation programs.** — Secure legal co‑management for 5 million km² of critical ecosystems and document the status of at least 100 threatened languages within those areas by 2030.
- **Implement mandatory biodiversity impact assessments for all large‑scale land‑use projects, with penalties for non‑compliance.** — All new projects over 10,000 hectares undergo assessment; compliance rate reaches 95% within 3 years.
