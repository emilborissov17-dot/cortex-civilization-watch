## Problem
Insufficient AI oversight and governance for municipal technology deployments in New York City

## Root Cause
Rapid adoption of AI tools outpaces existing regulatory frameworks and dedicated oversight bodies, leading to fragmented accountability and lack of standardized impact assessments

## Proposed Actions
- **Create a dedicated AI Oversight Office within NYC government that mandates AI Impact Assessments for all new AI systems before deployment** — 100% of AI projects launched after the office's creation have a completed, publicly available impact assessment within 30 days of launch
- **Pass a citywide ordinance requiring annual third‑party audits of existing AI systems used for public services** — All AI systems in operation receive at least one external audit per year, with audit reports posted on the city’s open data portal
