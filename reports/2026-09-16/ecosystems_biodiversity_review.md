# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-16

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Parachute science practices are leading to inadequate research and conservation of tropical insects, which comprise over 80% of insect species.

## Root Cause
Short-term, foreign-led studies that exclude local scientists result in data gaps, misaligned priorities, and ineffective conservation actions in tropical regions.

## Proposed Actions
- **Adopt a global framework for equitable research collaborations that requires local co‑leadership and data sharing for any tropical biodiversity project funded by major agencies.** — Increase the share of tropical insect studies with local lead authors from <30% to >70% within five years.
- **Establish and fund regional insect monitoring programs staffed primarily by local scientists and citizen scientists.** — Generate continuous population trend data for at least 50% of key tropical insect indicator groups within ten years.
