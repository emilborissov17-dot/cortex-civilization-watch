## Problem
Deployment of highly capable AI models (e.g., GPT‑6 Astra) into production systems at massive scale without independent safety verification

## Root Cause
Intense commercial pressure to outpace competitors and deliver new features, combined with the absence of enforceable, industry‑wide safety standards and oversight mechanisms

## Proposed Actions
- **Introduce mandatory, third‑party safety audits for any AI model exceeding a defined capability threshold before it can be used in critical or high‑traffic production environments** — 100 % of models classified as “high‑impact” have completed an independent audit and received a safety certification before public release
- **Create an open, cross‑industry safety benchmark suite (including alignment, robustness, and misuse testing) that is required for access to large‑scale compute resources** — Benchmark suite adopted by at least three leading AI labs and integrated into their deployment pipelines
- **Establish a global AI safety registry where deployments of models above the capability threshold are logged and periodically reviewed by an international oversight panel** — Registry contains entries for >90 % of high‑impact model deployments within two years
