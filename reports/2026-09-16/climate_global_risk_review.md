# CLIMATE GLOBAL RISK REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-16

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Proposed expansion of Heathrow Airport is projected to make aviation account for up to 80% of the United Kingdom's CO2 emissions by 2050.

## Root Cause
Policy decisions prioritize increased air traffic capacity without linking expansion to mandatory decarbonisation measures, combined with a lack of stringent carbon pricing and insufficient development of low‑carbon aviation technologies.

## Proposed Actions
- **Introduce a robust, revenue‑generating carbon price on aviation fuel that scales with emission intensity, and make it a prerequisite for any airport expansion consent.** — Limit aviation's share of UK CO2 emissions to no more than 30% by 2050, verified by annual emissions inventories.
- **Mandate a minimum percentage of sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) in all UK flights, targeting 20% SAF use by 2030 and 50% by 2040.** — Annual SAF blending ratio reported by airlines reaches the set targets.
- **Cap total annual flight movements at Heathrow to current levels until verifiable low‑carbon aircraft (e.g., electric, hydrogen, or advanced SAF‑compatible) achieve at least 50% market share.** — Flight movement cap enforced and reviewed annually; market share of low‑carbon aircraft reported.
