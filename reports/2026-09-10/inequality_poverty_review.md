## Problem
A majority of American households lack basic emergency savings, leaving them vulnerable to financial shocks and increasing poverty risk.

## Root Cause
Stagnant real wages, rising cost of living, and an inadequate social safety net combined with extreme wealth concentration that limits income mobility.

## Proposed Actions

- **Expand the Earned Income Tax Credit and Child Tax Credit to cover all workers earning below 200% of the median wage.** — Reduce the official US poverty rate by 2 percentage points within five years.
