# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-10

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Critical gaps in monitoring and protecting tropical insect biodiversity due to reliance on external "parachute" research and lack of coordinated global wildlife health governance.

## Root Cause
Scientific funding and conservation priorities are biased toward charismatic megafauna and are often led by non‑local researchers, resulting in insufficient data, capacity, and policy for the >80% of insect species that reside in tropical regions.

## Proposed Actions
- **Create a Global Wildlife Health Authority with a dedicated division for tropical insect health and biodiversity.** — Launch the authority and publish an annual global insect biodiversity status report within 2 years.
- **Implement a grant program that requires partnership with local scientists and institutions for any research on tropical insects.** — Increase the proportion of funded tropical insect projects led by local researchers from <10% to >50% within 3 years.
- **Develop a standardized, open‑access global insect monitoring network (e.g., citizen‑science + remote sensing).** — Achieve continuous coverage of >70% of tropical forested area for key indicator insect groups within 5 years.
