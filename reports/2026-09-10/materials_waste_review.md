# MATERIALS WASTE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-10

**Severity:** MEDIUM

## Problem
The recycling of plastic waste is not keeping up with the volume and complexity of material being generated, leading to a significant amount of plastic waste ending up in landfills or incinerators.

## Root Cause
Current sorting technology and processing costs are limiting the ability to recycle all types of plastics efficiently, resulting in only a fraction reaching true recycling stages.

## Proposed Actions
- **Develop advanced sorting technology that can efficiently process and recycle a wider range of plastics, including those with complex compositions.** — Increase the percentage of plastic waste recycled to at least 50% within two years.
- **Invest in research and development for new recycling methods that can handle difficult-to-recycle plastics, such as those found in electronic waste.** — Develop at least one new plastic recycling method by 2025.
