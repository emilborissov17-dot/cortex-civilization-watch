# LONG TERM FUTURE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-10

**Severity:** CRITICAL

## Problem
AI safety research and governance are underfunded and poorly coordinated relative to the rapid acceleration of advanced AI capabilities

## Root Cause
Market and corporate incentives prioritize speed and profit in AI development, while global governance, funding mechanisms, and coordination among safety researchers remain fragmented and weak

## Proposed Actions
- **Create an International AI Safety Consortium with binding safety standards that all major AI labs must adopt** — At least 90% of top-tier AI labs certified by the consortium within 3 years
- **Mandate that a minimum of 20% of all AI R&D budgets be allocated to independent safety research** — Verified budget reports showing the 20% allocation across the top 10 AI firms
- **Establish a global AI risk forecasting hub to continuously monitor capability progress and adjust safety investment levels** — Quarterly public risk assessment reports with actionable recommendations
