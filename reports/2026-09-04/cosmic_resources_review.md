## Problem
Absence of enforceable international regulations for space resource extraction, leading to potential unsustainable exploitation and conflict over celestial bodies.

## Root Cause
Rapid commercial interest and technological progress in asteroid mining outpace the development of binding legal frameworks and environmental safeguards under current space law.

## Proposed Actions
- **Negotiate and adopt a binding addendum to the Outer Space Treaty that defines sustainable extraction limits, benefit‑sharing mechanisms, and mandatory environmental impact assessments for space resource activities.** — Ratification by at least 30 space‑capable states within 3 years; establishment of a compliance monitoring registry.
- **Create an open‑access, satellite‑based monitoring system (leveraging hyperspectral imagers like Simera Sense) to track mining operations on near‑Earth asteroids and report data to an international registry.** — Deploy monitoring capability on at least two commercial missions within 2 years; publish quarterly transparency reports.
