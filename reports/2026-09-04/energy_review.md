## Problem
Rapid expansion of AI data centers is overloading U.S. power equipment and grid capacity, threatening reliable electricity supply and increasing reliance on fossil fuel generation.

## Root Cause
Exponential growth in AI compute demand combined with insufficient investment in grid modernization and renewable integration, plus lack of enforceable energy‑efficiency standards for large data centers.

- **Implement a carbon‑price floor that applies to electricity used by data centers exceeding a defined threshold.** — Reduce net‑zero‑aligned emissions from AI data‑center electricity use by 30% relative to 2026 baseline by 2030.
