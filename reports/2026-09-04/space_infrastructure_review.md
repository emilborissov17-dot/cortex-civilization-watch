## Problem
Rapid increase in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite constellations, especially Starlink, leading to orbital congestion and heightened collision/debris risk

## Root Cause
Commercial demand for global broadband combined with insufficient coordinated international regulation and lack of mandatory end‑of‑life disposal requirements

- **Create a global, real‑time orbital traffic management (OTM) platform managed by an impartial consortium of space agencies and industry stakeholders** — Operational OTM system covering >95% of active LEO assets within 3 years; documented reduction in predicted collision events by 40% within 5 years
