**Date:** 2026-09-29

## Problem
Regulatory uncertainty for asteroid and space resource mining due to lack of enacted legislation

## Root Cause
Political inertia and fragmented international governance have prevented the Space Resource Exploration and Utilization Act of 2015 from becoming law, leaving no clear legal framework for space resource extraction

## Proposed Actions
- **Create and adopt an international treaty on space resource utilization that defines ownership, licensing, and environmental safeguards** — Treaty signed by at least 30 space‑faring nations and ratified by 15 within 5 years
- **Establish a transparent, competitive licensing agency under the treaty to issue resource extraction permits** — License issuance process operational with clear criteria and public reporting within 3 years
