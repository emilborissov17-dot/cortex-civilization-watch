# INFRASTRUCTURE CITIES REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-29

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Opportunity zones are not reliably delivering equitable investment and economic development as intended.

## Root Cause
Policy design lacks clear, enforceable performance metrics and oversight, allowing private developers to capture benefits without demonstrable community impact.

## Proposed Actions
- **Mandate standardized, publicly audited impact reporting for every opportunity‑zone project, linking tax‑credit eligibility to measurable outcomes such as job creation for low‑income residents and affordable‑housing units delivered.** — At least 70% of certified projects meet predefined targets (e.g., 10% increase in low‑income employment within 3 years) within five years.
- **Create an independent oversight board with representation from affected communities, NGOs, and economic experts to review compliance and adjust incentives.** — Board issues quarterly compliance reports and recommends corrective actions for any project failing to meet targets.
