# ENERGY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-29

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Existing power grid infrastructure cannot reliably accommodate high penetration of intermittent renewable energy, leading to large-scale blackouts.

## Root Cause
Legacy grid designs were built for centralized, dispatchable generation; insufficient transmission capacity, lack of large‑scale storage, and slow regulatory updates prevent the system from handling variable solar and wind output.

## Proposed Actions
- **Launch a coordinated grid‑modernization program that upgrades transmission corridors, adds high‑capacity battery and pumped‑hydro storage, and implements smart‑grid control systems.** — Reduce the frequency of unplanned outages in high‑renewable regions by 70% within five years and increase renewable curtailment margin to <5% of total generation.
- **Create regulatory incentives (e.g., fast‑track permits, cost‑recovery mechanisms) for utilities to invest in flexible grid assets and demand‑response platforms.** — Achieve at least 30 GW of new flexible resources (storage, demand response) commissioned within three years.
