# ENERGY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-12

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Existing electricity transmission and distribution grids are not designed to accommodate the high variability and distributed nature of large-scale wind and solar generation, leading to curtailment and reliability issues.

## Root Cause
Legacy grid architecture built for centralized, dispatchable generation; insufficient investment and regulatory incentives for grid modernization, storage integration, and smart‑grid technologies.

## Proposed Actions
- **Adopt and enforce updated grid interconnection standards that require utilities to upgrade transmission capacity and implement advanced inverter controls for renewable farms.** — Reduce renewable curtailment rates to <5% within 5 years.
- **Create a dedicated public‑private fund for grid‑scale energy storage and smart‑grid pilots in high‑renewable regions.** — Deploy at least 10 GW of grid‑scale storage and achieve 20% peak‑load shaving in targeted zones by 2030.
- **Revise local zoning codes (e.g., expand agrivoltaic allowances beyond the current 1% cap) to accelerate distributed solar deployment combined with micro‑grid infrastructure.** — Increase installed distributed solar capacity in the jurisdiction by 30% within 3 years.
