## Problem
Massive underfunded reconstruction in Gaza combined with governance gaps is deepening poverty and inequality for millions of Palestinians

## Root Cause
Chronic conflict and political fragmentation prevent coordinated, adequately financed rebuilding, while external donors lack mechanisms to ensure effective local leadership and accountability

## Proposed Actions
- **Create a jointly governed Gaza Reconstruction Trust that pools the $71 bn pledged, ties disbursements to transparent milestones, and includes Palestinian NGOs, UN agencies, and donor representatives** — Release 30% of pledged funds within 12 months and complete 25% of critical infrastructure (water, housing, electricity) by end‑of‑year 2
- **Implement a targeted poverty‑reduction program that channels a portion of reconstruction funds into cash‑transfer and job‑creation schemes for the most vulnerable households** — Reduce the share of Gaza residents living below the poverty line from ~70% to <50% within 3 years
- **Establish an independent monitoring board (including local experts and international auditors) to publish quarterly progress reports and adjust allocations based on performance data** — Achieve ≥90% compliance with agreed milestones and public transparency scores above 80% on the board’s index
