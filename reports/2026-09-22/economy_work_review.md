# ECONOMY WORK REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-22

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Rapid expansion of data centers driven by AI and digital services is creating high energy consumption and environmental impact

## Root Cause
Market demand for ever greater compute capacity combined with weak regulatory standards and investment incentives that favor cheap electricity over sustainability

## Proposed Actions
- **Introduce mandatory Energy Usage Effectiveness (EUE) limits (e.g., PUE ≤ 1.3) for all new data‑center projects and retrofits** — Average PUE of newly commissioned data centers falls below 1.3 within 3 years
- **Provide tax credits and preferential financing for data centers that source ≥30 % of electricity from renewable sources** — 30 % of total data‑center electricity consumption in the region is renewable within 5 years
- **Create a public‑private “Data‑Center Climate Resilience” task force to develop best‑practice guidelines and monitor compliance** — Annual compliance report showing a year‑on‑year reduction in total data‑center energy intensity (kWh per compute unit) by at least 5 % after 5 years
