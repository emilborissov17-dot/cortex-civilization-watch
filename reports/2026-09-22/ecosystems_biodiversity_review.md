# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-22

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Authorization of new oil exploration projects in the Ecuadorian Amazon threatens pristine habitats and undiscovered species

## Root Cause
Economic priority of fossil‑fuel extraction combined with weak national and global governance on biodiversity protection

## Proposed Actions
- **Impose an immediate moratorium on all new oil concessions in Yasuni National Park and surrounding Amazonian areas, and launch a transparent multi‑stakeholder review process** — Number of new oil licenses issued drops to zero within 12 months; area of protected forest remains unchanged or expands by at least 10,000 km² in five years
- **Establish a Global Wildlife Health Authority to coordinate monitoring, data sharing, and rapid response to biodiversity threats** — Authority formally created and operating with at least 20 member states within three years; annual biodiversity risk reports published
