# MATERIALS WASTE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-22

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Recycling systems are unable to keep up with the rapid changes in packaging materials and waste streams, resulting in low recycling rates and increased environmental waste.

## Root Cause
Packaging is designed primarily for cost, marketing and convenience rather than recyclability, while regulations and infrastructure investments lag behind, creating a mismatch between waste generation and processing capacity.

## Proposed Actions
- **Adopt a nationwide ‘Recyclable‑by‑Design’ law that sets minimum recyclability criteria for all consumer packaging and requires clear, standardized labeling.** — Increase the national packaging recycling rate from current level to at least 55% within five years.
- **Fund and deploy AI‑driven sorting facilities at major waste processing hubs, prioritizing hazardous and mixed plastics.** — Achieve a 20% reduction in contamination of recyclable streams and a 15% rise in recovered material volume within three years.
- **Create financial incentives (tax credits, low‑interest loans) for companies that convert food waste to renewable natural gas (RNG) and for firms that build subsurface organic‑waste infrastructure.** — Add 10 GW of RNG capacity from food‑waste feedstock and 200 MW of subsurface organic‑waste processing capacity by 2030.
