## Problem
Renewable energy infrastructure is not keeping up with the growing demand for wind and solar power, leading to hidden infrastructure problems and potential power outages

## Root Cause
The existing power grid infrastructure was not designed to handle the variability of renewable energy sources, and upgrading it has not kept pace with the rapid growth of wind and solar power

- **Upgrade and expand the power grid infrastructure to accommodate the increasing use of wind and solar power** — Increase the capacity of the power grid to handle renewable energy sources by 20% within the next 2 years
