# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-08-13

**Severity:** CRITICAL

## Problem
Deforestation and habitat loss lead to significant biodiversity reduction, exemplified by the clearing of an area the size of a soccer field every minute in the Amazon basin

## Root Cause
Human activities such as agriculture, urbanization, and logging drive deforestation, disrupting delicate ecosystems and causing extinction debts

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement and enforce strict conservation policies and protected areas** — Achieve a 50% reduction in annual deforestation rates within the next 5 years
- **Promote sustainable land-use practices and reforestation efforts through education and incentives** — Increase global reforestation efforts by 20% annually for the next decade
