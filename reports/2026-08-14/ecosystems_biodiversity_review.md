# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-08-14

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Widespread biodiversity loss and ecosystem degradation, including deforestation and habitat destruction

## Root Cause
Human activities such as deforestation, land use changes, and pollution, driven by economic interests and lack of effective conservation policies

## Proposed Actions
- **Establish and expand protected areas, such as national parks and wildlife reserves** — Increase global protected area coverage by 30% within the next 5 years
- **Implement sustainable land use practices and certification schemes for commodities such as palm oil and soy** — Reduce deforestation rates by 50% within the next 10 years
