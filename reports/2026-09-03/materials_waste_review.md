# MATERIALS WASTE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-03

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Low effectiveness of current recycling and waste management systems leads to large amounts of plastic and hazardous waste (e.g., PFAS) being mis‑sorted, landfilled, or released into the environment.

## Root Cause
Fragmented collection standards, inadequate consumer guidance, insufficient investment in recycling infrastructure, and lack of market demand for high‑quality recycled materials.

## Proposed Actions
- **Adopt mandatory, uniform recycling labeling and contamination thresholds for all municipalities, coupled with a public education campaign.** — Increase the national plastic recycling rate from ~30% to ≥45% within five years and reduce contaminated load by 25%.
- **Implement Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) legislation requiring manufacturers to finance collection, sorting, and recycling of their packaging.** — By 2030, achieve 80% of post‑consumer plastic entering a certified recycling stream and create a market for ≥50% recycled-content products.
- **Fund R&D and scale‑up of high‑value circular‑economy technologies (e.g., polypropylene PCR, marine‑net filament conversion) through grants like the Clean Power Alliance Community Benefits Grant.** — Launch at least 10 pilot facilities producing certified recycled polymers or filament, delivering a combined 200,000 tonnes of recycled material per year by 2035.
