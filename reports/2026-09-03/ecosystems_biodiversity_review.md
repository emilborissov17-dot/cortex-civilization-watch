# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-03

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Human physical presence in natural areas is displacing wildlife and altering their movement patterns, contributing significantly to biodiversity loss.

## Root Cause
Unregulated expansion of human activities and lack of protected zones allow direct disturbance of habitats, making human presence a habitat-altering factor comparable to roads and cities.

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement legally binding no‑entry buffer zones around critical wildlife habitats and migration corridors, limiting recreational and commercial human traffic.** — Reduce documented wildlife displacement events by 30% within 5 years, measured through GPS tracking datasets.
- **Establish a Global Wildlife Health Authority with mandate to collect, analyze, and disseminate real‑time wildlife movement data and to issue guidance on human activity restrictions.** — Operational authority with at least 50 member nations and a standardized open‑access wildlife telemetry database within 3 years.
