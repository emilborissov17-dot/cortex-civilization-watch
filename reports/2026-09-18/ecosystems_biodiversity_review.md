# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-18

**Severity:** CRITICAL

## Problem
Rapid deforestation and habitat loss in tropical regions, especially the Amazon, are causing severe biodiversity decline.

## Root Cause
Commercial agriculture, illegal logging, and weak governance drive large‑scale forest clearing, while 'parachute science' neglects local ecosystems and community involvement.

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement and enforce a legally binding moratorium on new forest clearing in high‑biodiversity hotspots, coupled with payments for ecosystem services to Indigenous peoples.** — Reduce the Amazon deforestation rate by 40% within five years, measured by satellite monitoring (e.g., Global Forest Watch).
- **Create a coordinated global research framework that funds in‑situ tropical biodiversity studies led by local scientists, eliminating 'parachute science'.** — Increase the number of locally led biodiversity publications in tropical regions by 30% within three years.
