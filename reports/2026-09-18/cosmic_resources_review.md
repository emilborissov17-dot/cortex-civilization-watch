# COSMIC RESOURCES REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-18

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Absence of a binding international legal framework governing asteroid and other space resource extraction

## Root Cause
Fragmented national policies and the ambiguous interpretation of the Outer Space Treaty, combined with rapid commercial interest and insufficient consensus among major space‑faring nations

## Proposed Actions
- **Initiate a UN‑mandated International Space Resource Governance Treaty (ISRGT) drafting process** — Treaty text agreed upon and signed by at least 10 major space actors within 5 years
- **Create an independent Space Resource Oversight Board to require mandatory environmental impact assessments (EIA) for any off‑world mining mission** — 100 % of planned asteroid or lunar mining missions submit approved EIAs before launch
- **Establish a public, auditable registry of resource claims and extraction licenses linked to the ISRGT** — All commercial mining entities register claims; registry usage metrics reach 90 % compliance within 3 years
