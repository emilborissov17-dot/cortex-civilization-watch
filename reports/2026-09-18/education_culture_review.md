# EDUCATION CULTURE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-18

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
A significant proportion of students experience a reading comprehension plateau after third grade, leading to continued literacy struggles in later schooling.

## Root Cause
Curricula and teacher training focus heavily on basic decoding skills in early grades but neglect systematic development of higher‑order comprehension, vocabulary, and critical reading strategies for the middle elementary years.

## Proposed Actions
- **Adopt and scale evidence‑based literacy intervention frameworks (e.g., Reading Recovery, Guided Reading) specifically for grades 3‑5 across public schools.** — Increase the proportion of students meeting or exceeding grade‑level reading comprehension benchmarks by 15% within three years.
- **Implement mandatory, ongoing professional development for elementary teachers on instructional strategies for deep comprehension and critical thinking.** — At least 90% of teachers in target grades complete the program and demonstrate improved classroom practice as measured by classroom observation rubrics.
