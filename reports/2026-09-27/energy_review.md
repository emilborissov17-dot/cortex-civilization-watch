# ENERGY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-27

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Existing electricity grid infrastructure cannot accommodate the rapid increase in variable renewable energy (wind and solar), leading to reliability issues, curtailment, and limiting further renewable deployment.

## Root Cause
Legacy grid designs were built for centralized, dispatchable generation and have not been upgraded with smart‑grid, storage, and high‑capacity transmission technologies; regulatory and market structures also fail to incentivize flexibility and investment.

## Proposed Actions
- **Launch a coordinated national grid modernization program that funds transmission upgrades, high‑voltage DC interconnects, and smart‑grid technologies.** — Reduce renewable curtailment to below 2% of total generation by 2028.
- **Reform electricity market rules to value flexibility services and create clear revenue streams for battery storage, demand‑response, and flexible generation.** — Increase registered flexible capacity by 10 GW within five years.
- **Deploy pilot projects for high‑capacity, low‑loss HVDC corridors linking renewable‑rich regions to load centers, then scale successful pilots.** — Add at least 10 GW of HVDC transmission capacity by 2030.
