# WATER REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-27

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Record low water levels in major Western US reservoirs (Lake Powell and Lake Mead) indicating acute freshwater scarcity

## Root Cause
Prolonged drought and warming driven by climate change combined with over-allocation of water rights and high consumptive use in agriculture and urban areas

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement a tiered water allocation reform that prioritizes essential human and ecological needs, reduces overall allocations by 20%, and incentivizes water‑saving technologies for agriculture** — Increase average reservoir storage to at least 60% of capacity within 5 years
- **Mandate adoption of high‑efficiency drip irrigation and soil‑moisture monitoring on 50% of irrigated acreage in the Colorado River Basin** — Achieve a 15% reduction in agricultural water withdrawals from the basin within 3 years
