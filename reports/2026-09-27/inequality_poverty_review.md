## Problem
Gaza's post‑conflict reconstruction, estimated at $71 billion, is at high risk of failure due to fragmented local leadership and inadequate coordinated international funding, leading to prolonged poverty and widening inequality.

## Root Cause
Political fragmentation among Palestinian authorities combined with a lack of transparent, joint governance mechanisms for donor funds, which prevents effective allocation and implementation of reconstruction projects.

## Proposed Actions
- **Create a Gaza Reconstruction Authority (GRA) jointly chaired by recognized Palestinian representatives and an independent international oversight board to manage and audit all reconstruction funds.** — Achieve 30% of pre‑conflict housing and critical infrastructure (water, electricity, health facilities) restored within three years, verified by independent audits.
