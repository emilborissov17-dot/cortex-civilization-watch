# ENERGY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-08-08

**Severity:** MEDIUM

## Problem
Grid infrastructure has not kept up with the increasing adoption of renewable energy sources, leading to reliability and resiliency issues

## Root Cause
The existing power grid was designed for traditional energy sources and has not been adequately updated to accommodate the variable output of renewables

## Proposed Actions
- **Invest in grid modernization and infrastructure upgrades** — Increase the percentage of renewable energy sources in the grid without compromising reliability
- **Implement smart grid technologies and energy storage solutions** — Reduce the number of power outages and improve overall grid resilience
