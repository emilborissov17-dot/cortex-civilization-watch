# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-08-08

**Severity:** CRITICAL

## Problem
Biodiversity loss due to habitat destruction, climate change, and human activities

## Root Cause
Human activities such as deforestation, pollution, and over-exploitation of natural resources, leading to ecosystem disruption and species extinction

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement and enforce stricter environmental regulations and laws to protect ecosystems and wildlife** — Reduced rate of deforestation and habitat destruction, increased number of protected areas and species conservation
- **Launch public awareness campaigns to educate people about the importance of biodiversity and the impacts of human activities on the environment** — Increased public awareness and participation in environmental conservation efforts, reduced use of single-use plastics and pollutants
