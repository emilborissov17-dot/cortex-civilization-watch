## Problem
The lack of a binding, globally coordinated legal framework for asteroid and space resource mining creates regulatory uncertainty and risk of unsustainable, unregulated exploitation.

## Root Cause
National initiatives and private industry are moving ahead without an agreed international treaty; the 2015 U.S. Space Resource Exploration and Utilization Act never became law, leaving a governance gap.

## Proposed Actions
- **Negotiate and adopt a UN-led International Space Resource Utilization Treaty that includes sustainability criteria, transparent claim registration, and conflict‑resolution mechanisms.** — Treaty signed by at least 30 space‑faring nations and major private actors within 3 years; public registry with >80% of announced mining projects listed.
- **Fund a pilot asteroid‑mining demonstration that follows the treaty’s sustainability standards and publishes a full life‑cycle impact report.** — Successful extraction of ≥100 kg of water or metal with documented environmental impact metrics within 5 years.
