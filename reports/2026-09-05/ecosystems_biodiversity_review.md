# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-05

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Human presence and habitat destruction are reshaping wildlife movement patterns, fragmenting ecosystems and accelerating species decline.

## Root Cause
Large‑scale land‑use change driven by urban expansion, infrastructure development, and resource extraction that fragments habitats and forces wildlife into suboptimal areas.

## Proposed Actions
- **Create and legally protect wildlife corridors linking existing protected areas, and integrate corridor requirements into all new infrastructure and development plans.** — Increase the total length of functional corridors by 30% within 5 years and reduce measured displacement of tracked animal populations by 20% compared to baseline.
