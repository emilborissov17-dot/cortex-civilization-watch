# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-07-25

**Severity:** CRITICAL

## Problem
Deforestation and habitat loss are significantly reducing biodiversity, with an estimated area the size of a soccer field being clear-cut every minute in the Amazon basin

## Root Cause
Human activities such as agriculture, urbanization, and logging are driving deforestation and habitat destruction, largely due to lack of effective regulations and economic incentives

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement and enforce stricter regulations on deforestation and habitat destruction** — Reduce annual deforestation rate by 50% within the next 5 years
- **Promote sustainable land-use practices through education and economic incentives** — Increase the area of sustainably managed forests by 20% within the next 10 years
