## Problem
Extreme wealth and income inequality in the United States has caused the middle class to disappear and increased poverty levels

## Root Cause
Tax and fiscal policies that heavily favor capital over labor, deregulation of financial markets, and lack of robust redistributive mechanisms allow wealth to concentrate in the hands of a small elite while wages stagnate for the majority

## Proposed Actions
- **Enact a progressive wealth tax on net assets above $50 million, with rates scaling up to 3% for the ultra‑wealthy** — Reduce the U.S. Gini coefficient by at least 0.02 and increase the share of wealth held by the bottom 50% by 5% within five years
- **Raise the federal minimum wage to $15 per hour and index it to inflation** — Lift the median real hourly earnings of low‑wage workers by 10% and cut the official poverty rate by 2 percentage points within three years
- **Mandate corporate profit‑sharing programs (e.g., employee stock ownership plans) for companies with revenue over $1 billion** — Increase employee ownership stakes to at least 10% of total equity in targeted firms within five years
