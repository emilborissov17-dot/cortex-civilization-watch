# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-30

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
A proposed hydropower project in Montenegro's Komarnica Canyon threatens the habitat of two newly discovered snail species.

## Root Cause
Energy development plans are proceeding without adequate environmental impact assessments and with insufficient protection for critical micro‑habitats.

## Proposed Actions
- **Mandate a comprehensive, independent Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) that includes micro‑faunal surveys before any construction begins.** — EIA completed and publicly released within 12 months; project approval contingent on mitigation measures that preserve the canyon's habitat.
- **Designate the Komarnica Canyon area containing the new snail species as a protected biodiversity hotspot.** — Legal protection status granted within 24 months, with monitoring protocols established for the snail populations.
- **Develop and fund alternative renewable energy solutions (e.g., solar, wind) to offset the energy shortfall if the hydropower project is halted.** — At least 30 MW of alternative renewable capacity installed within 5 years, reducing reliance on the threatened site.
