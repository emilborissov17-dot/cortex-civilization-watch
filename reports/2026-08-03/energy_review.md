## Problem
The transition to renewable energy is hindered by the lack of infrastructure to support it, particularly in power grids that were not designed for wind and solar energy

## Root Cause
The existing power grid infrastructure was designed for traditional energy sources and has not kept pace with the growth of renewable energy, leading to inefficiencies and limitations in the transmission and distribution of renewable energy

## Proposed Actions
- **Invest in upgrading and expanding power grid infrastructure to support the integration of renewable energy** — Increase the percentage of renewable energy in the power grid by 20% within the next 5 years
