**Date:** 2026-08-06

## Problem
Inconsistent and fragmented AI integration policies in schools lead to unequal access to AI tools and uneven educational outcomes.

## Root Cause
Lack of coordinated national guidelines combined with reliance on individual teachers and local leaders to adopt AI, causing variability in resources, training, and implementation.

## Proposed Actions
- **Develop and mandate a unified AI competency framework for K-12 curricula, aligned with UNESCO guidelines.** — At least 80% of schools adopt the framework and report standardized AI lesson implementation within two years.
- **Launch a publicly funded, scalable teacher AI certification program with built‑in mentorship.** — Certify 70% of teachers nationwide in AI literacy within three years, tracked through certification records.
