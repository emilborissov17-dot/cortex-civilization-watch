# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-08-06

**Severity:** CRITICAL

## Problem
Widespread biodiversity loss and ecosystem destruction due to human activities such as deforestation, habitat loss, and climate change

## Root Cause
Human exploitation and neglect of natural resources, driven by economic interests and lack of effective conservation policies

## Proposed Actions
- **Establish and expand protected areas, such as national parks and wildlife reserves, to safeguard ecosystems and biodiversity** — Increase the total area of protected land and sea by 20% within the next 5 years
- **Implement sustainable land-use practices, such as reforestation and agroforestry, to reduce deforestation and promote ecosystem restoration** — Reduce global deforestation rates by 30% within the next 10 years
