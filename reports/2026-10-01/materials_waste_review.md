## Problem
Storms turning solar farms into sudden waste streams; plastic waste from marine sources being reused in various applications.

## Root Cause
Extreme weather can push thousands of young panels out of service at once, creating a costly reverse-logistics operation; plastic waste from marine sources is used for 3D printers and new products.

## Proposed Actions
- **Develop better solar panel durability standards and materials; improve insurance policies for renewable energy projects.** — Reduced number of damaged solar panels; lower insurance costs.
- **Promote recycling infrastructure and education to increase public awareness about plastic waste from marine sources.** — Higher rates of plastic waste recycling; reduced pollution in oceans.
