**Date:** 2026-10-01

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Record low water levels in Lake Powell and Lake Mead, jeopardizing water supply for the Colorado River Basin

## Root Cause
Prolonged drought and reduced mountain snowpack caused by climate change, combined with overallocation of river water rights and inefficient agricultural water use

## Proposed Actions
- **Negotiate a revised Colorado River Compact that caps withdrawals to sustainable flow levels and mandates tiered water‑use reductions during drought periods** — Lake Powell restored to at least 70 % of its design capacity and Lake Mead to at least 75 % within 10 years
- **Implement a large‑scale, incentive‑based program for precision irrigation and drought‑tolerant crops across the basin** — Average agricultural water use reduced by 15 % per acre‑foot within 5 years
