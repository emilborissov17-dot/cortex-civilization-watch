## Problem
Global ocean heat accumulation is being underestimated because marine heatwaves are reported as isolated extreme events while months of sustained elevated temperatures are ignored.

## Root Cause
Current climate monitoring systems prioritize short‑term extreme heatwave metrics and lack continuous, high‑resolution measurements of baseline ocean temperature, leading to systematic under‑reporting of total heat uptake.

## Proposed Actions
- **Deploy a global network of autonomous deep‑ocean profiling floats capable of continuous temperature recording and integrate their data into the official ocean heat‑content datasets.** — Reduce the uncertainty of global ocean heat uptake estimates from ~15% to <5% within five years and publish monthly baseline heat‑content reports.
