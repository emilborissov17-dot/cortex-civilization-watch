## Problem
Existing power grid infrastructure is not designed to handle the high penetration of renewable energy sources, leading to reliability, capacity, and security issues.

## Root Cause
Legacy grid designs were built for centralized, dispatchable fossil‑fuel plants; insufficient investment, regulatory lag, and lack of coordinated grid‑modernization policies have left the system unable to integrate variable wind and solar at scale.

## Proposed Actions
- **Deploy smart‑grid technologies (advanced SCADA, FACTS, wide‑area monitoring) and upgrade transmission capacity to match renewable growth targets.** — Reduce renewable curtailment to <5% and decrease frequency of grid‑stability incidents by 30% within 5 years.
- **Create regulatory incentives for large‑scale energy storage and demand‑response participation to provide grid flexibility.** — Achieve 20 GW of operational storage capacity dedicated to grid balancing by 2030.
