# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-08

**Severity:** CRITICAL

## Problem
Rapid deforestation in the Amazon basin is causing immediate, large‑scale loss of biodiversity and pushing the forest toward a savanna state.

## Root Cause
Economic incentives for land conversion to agriculture and cattle ranching combined with weak governance and lack of enforceable protection policies.

## Proposed Actions
- **Enact and enforce a moratorium on new forest clearance in high‑biodiversity areas of the Amazon, backed by satellite monitoring and penalties for violations.** — Reduce the rate of deforestation to below 0.5 km² per day within 2 years (≈10 % of current clear‑cutting rate).
- **Establish a Global Wildlife Health Authority to coordinate monitoring of wildlife disease and biodiversity indicators, and to trigger rapid response interventions.** — Authority fully operational with a reporting network covering at least 80 % of critical habitats worldwide within 5 years.
