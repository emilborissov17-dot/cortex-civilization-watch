# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-28

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Continual deforestation and habitat loss are causing rapid biodiversity decline in critical ecosystems such as the Amazon and the Komarnica Canyon in Montenegro.

## Root Cause
Economic incentives for land conversion (agriculture, logging, hydropower) combined with weak global governance and insufficient coordinated monitoring of wildlife health.

## Proposed Actions
- **Establish a legally binding global deforestation moratorium for primary forests, backed by satellite‑based real‑time monitoring and trade‑linked penalties.** — Reduce the rate of primary forest loss to less than 0.2% per year globally within five years (compared to the current ~0.5% per year).
- **Create a Global Wildlife Health Authority to coordinate disease surveillance, biodiversity assessments, and rapid response to habitat‑destructive projects.** — Operational authority with 100% coverage of high‑risk biodiversity hotspots (e.g., Amazon, Southeast Asia, Balkan river canyons) within three years.
