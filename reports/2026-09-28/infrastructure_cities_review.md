## Problem
Rising costs and regulatory bottlenecks are preventing cities from rapidly deploying affordable clean‑energy and smart‑city infrastructure.

## Root Cause
Fragmented permitting processes, policy uncertainty (e.g., sudden project cancellations), and insufficient dedicated funding for low‑income communities create financial and administrative barriers to sustainable projects.

## Proposed Actions
