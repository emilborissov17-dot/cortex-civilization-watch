## Problem
Extreme wealth inequality in the United States has eliminated the middle class and is driving a growing number of people into poverty.

## Root Cause
Tax and fiscal policies that heavily favor capital and top‑income earners, combined with weakened labor bargaining power and insufficient social safety nets, concentrate wealth among the top 1% while leaving wages stagnant for the majority.

## Proposed Actions
- **Pass a federal wealth tax on net assets above $50 million and raise marginal income tax rates for the top 1% to at least 45%.** — Reduce the U.S. Gini coefficient from ~0.49 to below 0.45 within five years.
- **Raise the federal minimum wage to $15 per hour and index it annually to inflation and productivity growth.** — Decrease the number of people living in poverty from 37.9 million to under 30 million within three years.
