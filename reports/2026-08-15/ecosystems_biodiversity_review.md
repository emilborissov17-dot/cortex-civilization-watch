# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-08-15

**Severity:** CRITICAL

## Problem
Deforestation and habitat loss are significantly reducing biodiversity, with the Amazon basin losing an area the size of a soccer field every minute and over half of the original six million square miles of rainforest already clear-cut

## Root Cause
Human activities such as agriculture and forestry are driving deforestation, largely due to economic and consumption pressures

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement and enforce stricter regulations on deforestation and habitat destruction** — Reduce deforestation rate by 50% within the next 5 years
- **Launch global education and awareness campaigns on the importance of biodiversity and the impact of human activities on ecosystems** — Increase public awareness by 30% within the next 2 years
