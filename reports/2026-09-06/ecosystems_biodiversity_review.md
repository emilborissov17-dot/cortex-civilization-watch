# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-06

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Human physical presence and land‑use expansion are reshaping wildlife movement patterns, fragmenting habitats and accelerating biodiversity loss.

## Root Cause
Uncoordinated expansion of settlements, infrastructure, and resource extraction without global wildlife health governance leads to habitat fragmentation and direct disturbance of animal populations.

## Proposed Actions
- **Establish a Global Wildlife Health Authority to set standards, share data, and coordinate mitigation measures for human‑wildlife interactions.** — By 2028, the authority will have ratified biodiversity impact assessment protocols adopted by at least 50% of signatory nations.
- **Implement wildlife corridors that reconnect fragmented habitats in high‑conflict regions.** — Increase the functional connectivity index of targeted corridors by 20% within five years, measured via GPS tracking of focal species.
- **Integrate biodiversity impact metrics into national land‑use planning, limiting new development in critical ecosystems.** — Reduce the rate of habitat loss in priority ecoregions by 15% by 2030, tracked through satellite land‑cover change analysis.
