# LONG TERM FUTURE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-17

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Significant resource gap between frontier AI labs and independent AI safety researchers limits effective oversight and mitigation of existential AI risks.

## Root Cause
Concentration of funding, talent, and compute resources in private AI labs, combined with limited and fragmented philanthropic and public funding for independent safety research.

## Proposed Actions
- **Establish a coordinated, multi‑year grant program (e.g., $500 M over 5 years) specifically for independent AI safety research institutions, with milestone‑based funding releases.** — Increase the number of independent safety research teams with >$10 M annual budget from <2 to >10 within three years; publish at least 20 peer‑reviewed safety papers per year.
- **Create a regulatory sandbox that requires frontier AI labs to allocate a minimum percentage (e.g., 5 %) of their R&D budget to external safety collaborations.** — Compliance reports from >80 % of labs within two years; documented joint safety projects initiated.
