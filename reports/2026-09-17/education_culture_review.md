# EDUCATION CULTURE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-17

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Students experience a sharp decline in reading comprehension after third grade, creating a 'missing middle' that hampers lifelong learning and equity.

## Root Cause
Curricula and teacher support are designed for early grades and high-tech novelty rather than sustained literacy development, leading to insufficient diagnostic assessment and targeted intervention in middle elementary years.

## Proposed Actions
- **Deploy evidence‑based, tiered reading intervention programs in grades 3‑5 across all public schools.** — Raise the percentage of students meeting grade‑level reading proficiency from the current baseline by at least 15% within three years.
- **Establish mandatory, ongoing literacy coaching for teachers of grades 3‑5, including training on diagnostic tools and culturally responsive instruction.** — Achieve 90% teacher participation and a 20% improvement in teacher self‑efficacy scores on literacy instruction surveys within two years.
- **Integrate regular, low‑stakes reading assessments to identify at‑risk learners before they reach fourth grade.** — Detect 95% of students who fall below proficiency by the end of third grade and place them into intervention tracks within one academic year.
