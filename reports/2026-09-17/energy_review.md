# ENERGY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-17

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Power grid infrastructure is insufficient to reliably integrate the rapidly growing amount of renewable energy generation.

## Root Cause
Historical grid designs were built for centralized fossil‑fuel plants; lack of coordinated investment, outdated regulations, and fragmented planning have prevented timely upgrades for flexibility, storage, and digital control.

## Proposed Actions
- **Launch a coordinated grid‑modernization program that upgrades transmission, adds high‑capacity storage, and deploys smart‑grid digital controls in regions with high renewable penetration.** — Reduce renewable curtailment by at least 30% and average outage duration by 40% within five years.
- **Amend grid codes to require new renewable projects to include onsite or contracted storage and demand‑response capabilities.** — 100% of new renewable installations comply with storage or demand‑response requirements within two years.
- **Create a national public‑private funding pool (e.g., green bonds, climate finance) dedicated to grid upgrades, targeting at least $50 billion of investment over ten years.** — Amount of capital mobilized and kilometers of upgraded transmission lines completed.
