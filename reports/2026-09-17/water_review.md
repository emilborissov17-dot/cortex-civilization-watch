# WATER REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-17

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Leaking TNT from a century-old German submarine wreck contaminates marine water and sediments

## Root Cause
Corrosion of the wreck's casings after >100 years releases stored explosives into the surrounding environment, and there is no systematic remediation program for historic munitions in marine settings

## Proposed Actions
- **Create a global registry of underwater munitions sites and prioritize removal or containment of those posing the highest toxic risk** — Register >90% of known hazardous wrecks and begin remediation on the top 10% within 5 years
- **Develop and deploy low-impact, remote-operated technologies to safely extract or neutralize TNT from wrecks** — Demonstrate successful neutralization of TNT at the German submarine site with <5% residual contamination in water and sediment after 2 years
