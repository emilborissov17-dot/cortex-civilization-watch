# ENERGY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-10-02

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Electric power grids are not being upgraded fast enough to handle rapidly growing demand and the increasing share of renewable generation, leading to capacity shortfalls and large-scale outages.

## Root Cause
Chronic under‑investment and regulatory lag in grid modernization, combined with legacy grid designs optimized for centralized fossil‑fuel plants rather than distributed renewable sources.

## Proposed Actions
- **Create a coordinated, publicly funded program to upgrade transmission and distribution infrastructure for renewable integration, including smart‑grid controls and large‑scale storage.** — Increase annual grid‑upgrade investment by 30% within 3 years and reduce average outage duration for renewable‑related events by 40% within 5 years.
- **Mandate grid codes that require utilities to plan for and accommodate at least 50% renewable penetration by 2035, with explicit timelines for reinforcement and digitalization.** — Compliance of 90% of grid operators with the new code by 2030, measured through regulatory reporting.
- **Establish a green‑bond market dedicated to financing grid‑modernization projects, with tax incentives for private investors.** — Raise $50 billion in dedicated grid‑modernization green bonds by 2027.
