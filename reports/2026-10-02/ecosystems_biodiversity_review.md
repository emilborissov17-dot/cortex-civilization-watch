# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-10-02

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Forests cover about 30% of the planet, but deforestation is clearing these forests at a rate that could result in their complete disappearance within 100 years; why should we care? Everything on Earth is interconnected and interdependent; biodiversity loss affects us too.

## Root Cause
deforestation; clear-cutting of forests on a massive scale, leading to loss of biodiversity and the world's rainforest potentially disappearing within 100 years.

## Proposed Actions
- **Reduce deforestation rates; promote sustainable forestry practices.** — Decrease the rate of forest clearing by 10% annually.
- **Raise awareness about the importance of biodiversity and the impacts of deforestation.** — Increase public knowledge on how to protect forests and biodiversity; measure through surveys or polls.
