**Date:** 2026-10-02

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
There is no binding international legal framework governing asteroid and other space resource extraction, leading to uncoordinated commercial activities and potential geopolitical conflict.

## Root Cause
Divergent national interests and the failure of proposed legislation (e.g., the 2015 U.S. Space Resource Exploration and Utilization Act) to become law have left a regulatory vacuum for space mining.

## Proposed Actions
- **Initiate a multilateral negotiation under COPUOS to draft an International Space Resource Utilization Treaty that defines property rights, environmental safeguards, and benefit‑sharing mechanisms.** — Draft treaty text completed and endorsed by at least 15 spacefaring nations within 3 years.
- **Create an independent oversight body to certify and monitor commercial space mining operations for compliance with the treaty.** — Operational oversight agency established and first certification granted within 5 years.
