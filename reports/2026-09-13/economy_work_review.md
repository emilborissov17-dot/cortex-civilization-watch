## Problem
Household budgets are increasingly strained by rising fuel-driven inflation, reducing affordability of essential goods and prompting low-quality substitutes (e.g., "skimpflation").

## Root Cause
Persistent high fuel prices caused by limited renewable energy deployment, geopolitical supply shocks, and inadequate price regulation, which feed into overall consumer price inflation.
