# LONG TERM FUTURE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-09

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Absence of a binding, global governance framework for the development and deployment of advanced AI systems, leading to uncoordinated risk mitigation and potential existential threats

## Root Cause
Competitive pressures and fragmented national policies drive AI labs to prioritize speed and market advantage over safety, while no enforceable international treaty exists to align standards and oversight

## Proposed Actions
- **Draft and adopt an International AI Safety Treaty that sets minimum safety, transparency, and audit requirements for frontier AI systems** — At least 30 sovereign states sign the treaty and commit to annual independent AI safety audits within 3 years
- **Create a global AI risk registry funded by a coalition of governments and NGOs, where high‑risk AI projects must be registered and undergo peer review before deployment** — Register >90% of AI projects classified as “high‑risk” (e.g., >10B parameters) and achieve 80% compliance with peer‑review recommendations within 5 years
