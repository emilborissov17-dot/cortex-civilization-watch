# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-09

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
The world lacks a coordinated Global Wildlife Health Authority, resulting in delayed detection and response to wildlife disease outbreaks that threaten biodiversity.

## Root Cause
Fragmented international governance, insufficient funding, and poor data sharing among nations and institutions prevent the establishment of a unified body to monitor and manage wildlife health.

## Proposed Actions
- **Establish a Global Wildlife Health Authority under the UN system with a legal mandate for disease surveillance and rapid response.** — Operational wildlife disease reporting network covering 80% of biodiverse hotspots within 3 years; reduction of time from outbreak detection to response by 50% within 5 years.
- **Create an open-access, real‑time data platform linking national wildlife agencies, research institutions, and NGOs.** — At least 1000 active data contributors and 10,000 verified wildlife health records uploaded within 2 years.
