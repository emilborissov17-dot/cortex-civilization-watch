## Problem
Existing power grid infrastructure cannot handle the rapid increase of wind and solar generation, leading to bottlenecks and reliability issues.

## Root Cause
Grids were originally built for centralized, dispatchable fossil‑fuel plants and have not been upgraded with smart controls, storage, and new transmission capacity, while investment and regulatory frameworks lag behind renewable growth.

## Proposed Actions
- **Launch a coordinated grid‑modernization program that upgrades at least 30% of critical transmission corridors and installs advanced digital control systems to accommodate variable renewables.** — Reduce renewable curtailment by 50% and improve SAIDI (System Average Interruption Duration Index) by 20% compared to baseline.
- **Implement nationwide demand‑response and distributed‑storage incentives to provide flexible load that can absorb intermittent generation.** — Enroll 10% of residential and commercial electricity customers in demand‑response programs within 5 years.
