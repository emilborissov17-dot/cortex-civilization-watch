# COSMIC RESOURCES REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-09

## Problem
Absence of a clear, enforceable legal and governance framework for asteroid and other space resource extraction

## Root Cause
Rapid private-sector expansion outpaces national and international legislation; the 2015 US Space Resource Exploration and Utilization Act never became law, and no binding global treaty exists yet

## Proposed Actions
- **Negotiate and adopt an international Space Resource Sustainability Treaty that defines ownership rights, environmental safeguards, and transparent reporting requirements** — Treaty signed by at least 30 spacefaring nations and two major private industry coalitions within five years
- **Establish a global Space Resource Oversight Agency under the UN to certify extraction projects and monitor environmental impact** — Agency operational with annual public resource extraction and impact reports by year three
