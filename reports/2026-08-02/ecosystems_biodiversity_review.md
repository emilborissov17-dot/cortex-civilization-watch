# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Habitat loss and pesticide use are causing significant decline in bumblebee populations

## Root Cause
Human activities such as deforestation, urbanization, and intensive agriculture are leading to habitat destruction and increased use of pesticides

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement integrated pest management practices that reduce pesticide use** — Reduce pesticide use by 20% within the next 2 years
- **Create and restore habitats for bumblebees and other pollinators** — Increase pollinator-friendly habitats by 30% within the next 5 years
