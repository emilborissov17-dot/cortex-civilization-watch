# ENERGY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-11

**Severity:** MEDIUM

## Problem
The Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative (RGGI) is not effectively reducing electricity bills despite investments in clean energy.

## Root Cause
Electricity demand growth in the Northeast and mid-Atlantic region is outpacing RGGI's funding for renewable energy, leading to higher electricity prices.

## Proposed Actions
- **Increase the funding for renewable energy sources by states participating in RGGI to match or exceed electricity demand growth.** — Reduce electricity bills by at least 10% within a year
