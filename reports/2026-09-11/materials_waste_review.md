**Date:** 2026-09-11

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Low recycling rates for mixed and multi-layer plastic packaging, causing high landfill disposal

## Root Cause
Fragmented regulations, lack of standardized labeling, insufficient economic incentives for recyclers, and limited advanced sorting infrastructure

## Proposed Actions
- **Adopt a mandatory, globally‑harmonized labeling system for all plastic packaging that specifies material composition and recyclability, coupled with an EPR scheme that charges producers based on the recyclability of their packaging** — Increase the proportion of mixed/ multi‑layer plastic that is successfully sorted and recycled from current ~15% to at least 30% within 5 years
