# MATERIALS WASTE REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-21

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Current packaging waste exceeds the capacity of existing recycling systems, leading to high landfill and incineration rates and environmental contamination.

## Root Cause
Packaging materials have shifted to multi‑layer, mixed‑polymer designs that are not compatible with conventional recycling streams, combined with weak regulatory incentives and insufficient producer responsibility.

## Proposed Actions
- **Implement an Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) scheme for all packaged goods, requiring manufacturers to meet recyclable‑by‑design targets and fund collection/processing.** — Achieve a 20% increase in the recycling rate of packaging waste and a 15% reduction in landfill disposal of packaging by 2029.
- **Allocate targeted public grants (similar to Massachusetts' $2.8 M waste‑reduction grants) to develop and scale advanced material‑sorting and chemical recycling technologies.** — Deploy at least three pilot advanced recycling facilities handling mixed‑polymer packaging, processing a combined 500 000 tons of waste per year within five years.
