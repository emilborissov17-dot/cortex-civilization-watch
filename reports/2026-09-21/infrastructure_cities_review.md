## Problem
Insufficient trained workforce to support emerging urban air mobility (air taxi) services

## Root Cause
Lack of coordinated education, certification programs, and industry standards for air taxi pilots, maintenance, and operations, combined with limited public investment in specialized training infrastructure

## Proposed Actions
- **Establish a national Air Mobility Workforce Development Initiative that funds accredited training pipelines for pilots, engineers, and ground staff, with mandatory certification aligned to FAA UAM guidelines** — Enroll 5,000 students in certified air‑taxi programs within three years and achieve 80% job placement in UAM operators
- **Create public‑private partnership grants for regional pilot projects (e.g., the California program) to replicate successful training models nationwide** — Launch at least five regional training hubs across the US within two years
