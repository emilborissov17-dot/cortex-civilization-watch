## Problem
Large quantities of valuable rare earth elements (REEs) remain locked in coal ash, red mud, and mine tailings, causing resource waste and environmental contamination.

## Root Cause
Current waste management and extraction practices lack scalable, low‑impact technologies for REE recovery, and regulatory frameworks do not incentivize or mandate resource extraction from these wastes.

## Proposed Actions
