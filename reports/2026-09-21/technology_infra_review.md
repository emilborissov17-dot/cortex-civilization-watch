**Date:** 2026-09-21

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
The rapid proliferation of AI tools and data center expansion is widening the digital divide, leaving a large portion of the global population without access to essential digital infrastructure and AI services.

## Root Cause
Unequal investment in broadband and energy infrastructure, combined with commercial AI products that assume existing connectivity and prioritize profit over universal access.

## Proposed Actions
- **Launch a publicly funded global broadband program financed by a carbon‑pricing levy, with mandates for renewable‑energy‑powered data center construction in underserved regions.** — Increase the share of the global population with reliable broadband from ~48% to 70% and ensure at least 30% of new data center capacity runs on renewable energy within five years.
- **Require AI service providers to offer a free, privacy‑preserving tier for essential educational and health applications in low‑income markets.** — At least 10 million active low‑income users access free AI tools for education or health within two years.
