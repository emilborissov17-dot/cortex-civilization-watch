# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-02

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Human physical presence in natural areas is altering wildlife movement and habitat use as strongly as built infrastructure

## Root Cause
Unregulated human access and expansion into wildlife habitats creates disturbance, fragmenting ecosystems and forcing animals to change routes

## Proposed Actions
- **Establish and enforce buffer zones and limited‑access corridors in key wildlife habitats, using GPS monitoring to restrict human entry** — Reduce deviation of tracked animal movement paths from baseline by at least 30% within five years
- **Implement a global wildlife movement monitoring program to identify hotspots where human presence most disrupts species** — Publish annual hotspot maps and achieve a 20% decrease in human‑induced disturbance events in identified hotspots within three years
