The DOT proposal to streamline land leases along roads and railroads for utility use may reduce community control over public right‑of‑way space, threatening green infrastructure and equitable access.

## Root Cause
Regulatory incentives favor rapid utility expansion and revenue generation, while community participation and environmental safeguards are weak in current lease processes.
