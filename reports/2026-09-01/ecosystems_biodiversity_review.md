# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-01

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Accelerating global deforestation is causing rapid loss of habitats and biodiversity.

## Root Cause
Unsustainable land‑use driven by expansion of agriculture, logging, and consumer demand for cheap, non‑sustainable products.

## Proposed Actions
- **Adopt and enforce legally binding forest‑protection targets (e.g., zero net deforestation by 2030) coupled with certification schemes for sustainable sourcing.** — Global forest loss reduced to <5 million hectares per year and forest cover increased by 2 % by 2030.
- **Implement large‑scale payment‑for‑ecosystem‑services programs that compensate local communities for maintaining forest cover.** — At least 50 % of high‑risk forest regions enrolled in PES schemes within 5 years.
- **Launch a coordinated public‑awareness campaign linking consumer choices to deforestation impacts, promoting certified sustainable products.** — Increase share of certified sustainable products in major markets to 30 % within 3 years.
