# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-14

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Rapid deforestation and habitat loss in tropical forests (e.g., the Amazon) are causing immediate biodiversity decline and creating long‑term extinction debt.

## Root Cause
Economic incentives for agriculture, logging, and extractive industries combined with weak governance and insufficient inclusion of local/tropical scientists in conservation planning.

## Proposed Actions
- **Adopt and enforce a legally binding deforestation‑free supply‑chain certification for major agricultural commodities, linked to public procurement and trade agreements.** — Reduce the area cleared per minute in the Amazon by 50% within five years, measured via satellite forest‑cover monitoring (e.g., Global Forest Watch).
- **Establish and fund community‑led protected area networks in high‑biodiversity tropical regions, with guaranteed tenure for Indigenous peoples.** — Increase legally protected forest area by 10 million hectares within three years, tracked through national protected‑area registries.
