# ENERGY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-14

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Electrical grid infrastructure is insufficient for the high penetration of renewable generation, causing reliability issues and curtailment.

## Root Cause
Legacy grid designs were built for centralized, dispatchable power plants and have not been upgraded with modern transmission, distribution, and smart‑grid technologies needed to handle variable wind and solar output.

## Proposed Actions
- **Launch a coordinated, multi‑year national program to modernize transmission corridors, install high‑capacity FACTS devices, and deploy advanced distribution‑level smart‑inverter controls.** — Reduce renewable curtailment to <5% of installed capacity and lower reported grid congestion events by 30% within three years.
- **Create a performance‑based subsidy for utility‑scale and distributed energy storage linked to grid‑balancing services.** — Add at least 20 GW of usable storage capacity by 2028, measured by installed MW‑h of certified storage that participates in ancillary service markets.
