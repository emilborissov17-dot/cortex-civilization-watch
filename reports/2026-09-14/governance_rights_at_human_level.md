# GOVERNANCE RIGHTS AT HUMAN LEVEL
**Date:** 2026-09-14

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
State actors are using criminal prosecutions, passport cancellations, and unfair trials to silence political dissent and Indigenous leadership.

## Root Cause
Weak rule of law combined with authoritarian or semi‑authoritarian governance structures that prioritize regime security over civil liberties.

## Proposed Actions
- **Create a coordinated international advocacy coalition to issue targeted diplomatic statements and conditional aid tied to the release of political prisoners and the restoration of administrative rights (e.g., passports).** — Reduction of at least 50 % in reported cases of passport revocation or politically motivated prosecutions within 12 months.
- **Establish and fund regional legal defense funds for Indigenous authorities, opposition politicians, and human‑rights defenders in the affected countries.** — Successful filing of appeals or injunctions in at least 3 high‑profile cases per year, resulting in either case dismissals or stays of execution.
- **Push UN Human Rights Council to adopt a resolution calling for immediate suspension of administrative measures (passport cancellations) used as repression tools, with a monitoring mechanism.** — Adoption of the resolution and quarterly reporting on compliance by the target states.
