# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-15

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Parachute science practices are leading to inadequate conservation of tropical insect biodiversity, which comprises over 80% of insect species.

## Root Cause
Research funding and academic incentives favor short‑term, externally led studies that exclude local scientists, resulting in limited capacity building, data gaps, and misaligned conservation priorities in tropical regions.

## Proposed Actions
- **Create mandatory co‑leadership grant requirements that pair foreign researchers with local institutions and allocate a minimum percentage of funds to local capacity building and data sharing.** — By 2027, 80% of funded tropical insect projects will have documented local co‑leadership and a publicly accessible data repository.
- **Establish a Global Tropical Insect Monitoring Network coordinated by the IUCN, providing standardized protocols and open‑access databases.** — Baseline population trends for at least 50% of key tropical insect taxa collected within three years of network launch.
