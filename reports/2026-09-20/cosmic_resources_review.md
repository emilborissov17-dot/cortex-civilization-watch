# COSMIC RESOURCES REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-20

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Absence of a binding, internationally coordinated legal and governance framework for asteroid and other space resource extraction

## Root Cause
Fragmented national initiatives, outdated space law (Outer Space Treaty does not address resource ownership), and commercial pressure have prevented the creation of a unified, sustainable regulatory regime

## Proposed Actions
- **Negotiate and adopt an International Space Resource Sustainability Treaty that defines licensing, environmental impact assessment, and benefit‑sharing rules for asteroid mining** — Treaty signed by at least 30 space‑faring countries and major private actors within five years; establishment of a global registry with >80% of planned extraction missions reported
