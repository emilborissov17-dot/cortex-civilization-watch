## Problem
Mismatch between modern packaging materials and existing recycling infrastructure leads to increased landfill and incineration waste.

## Root Cause
Packaging design and material composition have evolved faster than recycling system capabilities, and there is insufficient regulatory pressure on producers to use recyclable materials.

- **Implement an Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) scheme that mandates recyclable or compostable packaging and funds recycling infrastructure upgrades.** — Increase the percentage of packaging material recycled from current ~40% to at least 60% within 5 years.
- **Standardize a limited set of packaging polymers and require clear labeling to simplify sorting.** — Reduce contamination rates in recycling streams by 30% within 3 years.
