**Date:** 2026-09-20

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Significant digital divide: large populations lack reliable, affordable internet access and digital devices.

## Root Cause
Historical underinvestment and policy neglect in broadband infrastructure, especially in low‑income, rural, and formerly colonized regions, combined with market-driven deployment that prioritizes profitable areas.

## Proposed Actions
- **Establish a universal broadband fund financed by a modest levy on high‑speed services to subsidize fiber or 5G rollout in underserved areas.** — Increase broadband penetration in target low‑access regions to at least 80% of households within 5 years.
- **Create a public‑private partnership to manufacture and distribute low‑cost, rugged tablets pre‑loaded with open‑source educational content.** — Distribute 10 million devices to schools and community centers in the identified gap areas within 3 years.
