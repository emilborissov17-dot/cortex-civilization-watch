# ECOSYSTEMS BIODIVERSITY REVIEW
**Date:** 2026-09-20

**Severity:** HIGH

## Problem
Accelerating deforestation in tropical regions is causing rapid loss of insect biodiversity, undermining ecosystem stability.

## Root Cause
Economic incentives for land conversion combined with weak protection policies and research practices that exclude local scientists ('parachute science') prevent effective conservation.

## Proposed Actions
- **Expand and legally enforce protected areas in the most biodiversity‑rich tropical forests, with co‑management by local communities.** — Achieve a 30% reduction in annual deforestation rate in targeted regions within 5 years, measured by satellite forest‑cover data.
- **Launch a global program to replace 'parachute science' with locally led research partnerships, providing grant funding and capacity‑building for scientists in tropical countries.** — Increase the share of research led by local institutions to 60% of tropical biodiversity studies within 3 years.
- **Implement payment‑for‑ecosystem‑services schemes that compensate landholders for preserving forest cover.** — Enroll at least 500,000 hectares of forest under PES contracts within 5 years, tracked through national land‑registry reports.
